**Hospital Operation Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI**


**Project Overview**

This project analyzes hospital operational data using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate patient admissions, readmissions, waiting time, treatment costs, and treatment outcomes.

The dashboard provides an overview of hospital performance through interactive visualizations, helping users explore patient demographics, insurance coverage, diagnosis patterns, severity levels, and treatment outcomes.


**Objectives**

Analyze the number of admitted and readmitted patients.

Examine average patient waiting time and average treatment cost.

Track patient admissions across different years.

Understand the distribution of patients by gender and insurance type.

Evaluate treatment outcomes and diagnosis severity levels.

Compare patient counts across different medical diagnoses.


**Tools Used**

Microsoft Power BI — Data visualization and dashboard development.

Power Query — Data transformation and preparation.

DAX — Measures and KPI calculations, where applicable.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

The dashboard highlights four key performance indicators:

Total Admitted Patients: Approximately 100K

Total Readmitted Patients: Approximately 7K

Average Wait Time: 3 hours

Average Treatment Cost: Approximately $2K


**Dashboard Visualizations**

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

Number of Patients by Year — Tracks patient admissions across 2024, 2025, and 2026.

Patients by Insurance Type — Shows the distribution of patients across Government, Employer, Private, and Self-Pay insurance categories.

Patients by Gender — Compares the number of male and female patients.

Patients by Treatment Outcome — Examines the proportions of patients who recovered, improved, were transferred, or deceased.

Diagnosis by Severity Level — Displays patient distribution across Low, Medium, High, and Critical severity levels.

Patients by Diagnosis — Compares patient counts across medical diagnoses, including heart disease, stroke, cancer, hypertension, and pneumonia.


**Key Insights**

The dashboard records approximately 100,000 admitted patients and 7,000 readmitted patients.

Average patient waiting time is approximately 3 hours.

The average treatment cost is displayed as approximately $2K.

Male and female patients appear almost equally represented, at approximately 50K each.

Insurance coverage is distributed relatively evenly across the four insurance categories.

The largest patient group falls under the Low severity case category, followed by Medium, High, and Critical.

The treatment outcome chart indicates that recovery accounts for the largest share of recorded outcomes.

Heart disease was the most common patient diagnosis.
